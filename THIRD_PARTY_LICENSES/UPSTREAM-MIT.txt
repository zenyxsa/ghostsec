GhostSec adapts selected reconnaissance tool-wrapper and public-search/page-fetch
functionality inspired by the upstream tools.py and search.py modules. These
components have been modified for GhostSec's registry, scope controls, error
handling, and public-only page-fetch policy. The rest of GhostSec is maintained
as a separate project. The upstream license text below is retained in full.
